## Why it moved

A skill published from two repositories drifts, and this one had. The copy that
used to live here was frozen in 2026-07 and was missing the executable `tokens contrast` command.

Keeping it installable would have meant running the old behaviour while reading
the current documentation — so this page now points at the maintained version
instead of shipping a second one.
