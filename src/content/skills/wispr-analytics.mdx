## What it does

Analyzes your Wispr Flow voice dictation history to surface work patterns, communication habits, energy levels, and mental health signals. Also manages the Wispr Flow dictionary — adding recognition terms, fixing recurring mishears, and suggesting improvements based on ASR vs formatted text divergence.

## Key features

- **Four analysis modes** — Technical (coding focus), Soft (communication), Trends (volume/frequency), Mental (sentiment/energy)
- **Bilingual support** — Handles Russian/English code-switching naturally
- **Comparison dashboards** — Chart.js visual comparisons between periods with hourly activity heatmaps
- **Dictionary management** — Export, import, add, remove, and suggest dictionary corrections
- **Proactive suggestions** — Detects recurring ASR mismatches and recommends dictionary entries
- **Auto-restart** — Restarts Wispr Flow after dictionary import operations

## When to use

When you want periodic self-reflection on work habits, energy patterns, or communication style. Also when managing Wispr Flow's dictionary — adding terms, fixing mishears, or analyzing dictation quality.
